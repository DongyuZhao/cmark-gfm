## Formula option gates

These examples attach the formula extension without enabling dollar formula
delimiters. Dollar-delimited syntax remains ordinary Markdown.

```````````````````````````````` example
Inline $x+y$ end.
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <paragraph>
    <text xml:space="preserve">Inline $x+y$ end.</text>
  </paragraph>
</document>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
$$
x+y
$$
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <paragraph>
    <text xml:space="preserve">$$</text>
    <softbreak />
    <text xml:space="preserve">x+y</text>
    <softbreak />
    <text xml:space="preserve">$$</text>
  </paragraph>
</document>
````````````````````````````````

Formula fenced-code blocks remain enabled by the formula extension.

```````````````````````````````` example
```formula
x+y
```
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <formula_block />
</document>
````````````````````````````````
