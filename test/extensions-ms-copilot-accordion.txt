## MS Copilot accordion

The MS Copilot accordion extension recognizes `details` elements with a
`summary` child. The `summary` content is parsed into a `header` child, and the
remaining `details` content is parsed into a `content` child.

```````````````````````````````` example
<details>
<summary>Title</summary>
Body
</details>
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <ms-copilot-accordion>
    <header>
      <text xml:space="preserve">Title</text>
    </header>
    <content>
      <paragraph>
        <text xml:space="preserve">Body</text>
      </paragraph>
    </content>
  </ms-copilot-accordion>
</document>
````````````````````````````````

Summary content is parsed as inline markdown, and details content is parsed as
block markdown.

```````````````````````````````` example
<details>
<summary>**Title** text</summary>
Before

- one
- two
</details>
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <ms-copilot-accordion>
    <header>
      <strong>
        <text xml:space="preserve">Title</text>
      </strong>
      <text xml:space="preserve"> text</text>
    </header>
    <content>
      <paragraph>
        <text xml:space="preserve">Before</text>
      </paragraph>
      <list type="bullet" tight="true">
        <item>
          <paragraph>
            <text xml:space="preserve">one</text>
          </paragraph>
        </item>
        <item>
          <paragraph>
            <text xml:space="preserve">two</text>
          </paragraph>
        </item>
      </list>
    </content>
  </ms-copilot-accordion>
</document>
````````````````````````````````

Nested `details` elements are parsed as nested accordions in content.

```````````````````````````````` example
<details>
<summary>Outer</summary>
Intro
<details>
<summary>Inner</summary>
Nested body
</details>
Outro
</details>
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <ms-copilot-accordion>
    <header>
      <text xml:space="preserve">Outer</text>
    </header>
    <content>
      <paragraph>
        <text xml:space="preserve">Intro</text>
      </paragraph>
      <ms-copilot-accordion>
        <header>
          <text xml:space="preserve">Inner</text>
        </header>
        <content>
          <paragraph>
            <text xml:space="preserve">Nested body</text>
          </paragraph>
        </content>
      </ms-copilot-accordion>
      <paragraph>
        <text xml:space="preserve">Outro</text>
      </paragraph>
    </content>
  </ms-copilot-accordion>
</document>
````````````````````````````````
