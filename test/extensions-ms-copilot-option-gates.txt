## MS Copilot option gates

These examples attach the MS Copilot extensions without enabling their parser
options. The extension syntax remains ordinary Markdown/HTML.

```````````````````````````````` example
Cite 【smith-2024】 today.
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <paragraph>
    <text xml:space="preserve">Cite 【smith-2024】 today.</text>
  </paragraph>
</document>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
Meet <Person>Aman</Person> today.
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <paragraph>
    <text xml:space="preserve">Meet </text>
    <html_inline xml:space="preserve">&lt;Person&gt;</html_inline>
    <text xml:space="preserve">Aman</text>
    <html_inline xml:space="preserve">&lt;/Person&gt;</html_inline>
    <text xml:space="preserve"> today.</text>
  </paragraph>
</document>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
<details>
<summary>Title</summary>
Body
</details>
.
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE document SYSTEM "CommonMark.dtd">
<document xmlns="http://commonmark.org/xml/1.0">
  <html_block xml:space="preserve">&lt;details&gt;
&lt;summary&gt;Title&lt;/summary&gt;
Body
&lt;/details&gt;
</html_block>
</document>
````````````````````````````````
